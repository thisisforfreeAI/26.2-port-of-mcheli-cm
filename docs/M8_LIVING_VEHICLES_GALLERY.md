# M8 living vehicle gallery

Native, unedited Minecraft screenshots of production renderers in controlled cosmetic preview states. These are development fixtures, not combat captures. Each stage has three stock-vehicle examples. Some restrained animation, shimmer and sound cannot be assessed from still images.

[Implementation and limits](M8_LIVING_VEHICLES.md). Target: 60 FPS on RTX 3060 8GB / Ryzen 7 7800X3D at 1080p; target-PC performance is not measured here.
