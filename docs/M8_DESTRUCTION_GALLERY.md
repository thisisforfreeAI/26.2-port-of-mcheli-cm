# M8 destruction effects gallery

24 native screenshots: three vehicles for each of the eight effects. Click a picture to open it at full size.

Native Minecraft screenshots on stock vehicle models. Controlled cosmetic states supply health/hit events to the production renderers; these are unedited in-game previews, not combat captures or generated mockups.

[Effect descriptions and limits](M8_DESTRUCTION_EFFECTS.md).

Target: 60 FPS on RTX 3060 8GB / Ryzen 7 7800X3D at 1080p. Target-PC FPS has not been measured.
